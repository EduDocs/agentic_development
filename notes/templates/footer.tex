%% Deliberately empty. It exists so that anything which must close the document
%% has a declared home: a colophon, an acknowledgements block, a printing

%%

%% \bibliography and \end{document}. The bibliography is absent here by
%% decision, not by oversight: these notes cite author-year inline and close
%% each chapter with an annotated Further Reading list, so nothing is \cite'd
%% and the build skips bibtex entirely (resources/STYLE.md, "Citations"). If
%% that decision is ever reversed, this is where the bibliography goes.
%%
%% The trailing % on the last line is the house rule for every \input'ed file:
%% without it the file's final line ending emits a stray space. See
