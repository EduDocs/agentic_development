# nano-evolve strategies (LaTeX paper authoring)
#
# One line per branch. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.
# run.sh expects at least N_BRANCHES (default 4) non-empty lines.
#
# Each branch authors the SAME manuscript from a different editorial bias, so
# the population explores the paper's design space rather than converging on
# one editor's habits. A bias names (a) which REGISTER the branch tends to
# push — the .md conceptual spine or the .tex shipped prose — and (b) what kind
# of move it favors. Read PROGRESSION.md for the .md <-> .tex sidecar model.

spine-exploration (.md-led): sketch theorems and promising directions in the sidecars first, harmonize the conceptual spine across sections, and only realize into prose once a direction is solid (lean on /md2tex)
prose-crystallization (.tex-led): take the settled spine and write/sharpen the shipped prose — tighten statements, complete proofs, fix definitions — then feed concept changes back with /tex2md
novelty-and-positioning: strengthen the contribution and its framing — sharpen what is new, position against prior work, add and wire up citations in references.bib (ground in resources/ papers)
grounding-and-rigor: pull supporting results and techniques from the resources/ arXiv library to shore up proofs and assumptions, close logical gaps, and make every claim checkable
