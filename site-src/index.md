# Agentic Development in Research

### → Agentic Evolutionary Frameworks

A project-based graduate course on building agentic AI systems for research, culminating in
**agentic evolutionary frameworks that can accelerate discovery**. Students move from *operating* a
professional agentic rig, to *building* research agents, to an evolutionary search that discovers
a model from data in their own field.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 3-credit · 14 weeks × 3h + a week-15 finals slot · project-based |
| **Audience** | Graduate students, cross-disciplinary |
| **Prerequisites** | Some Python; **no** assumed background in agents, git, or the shell |
| **Substrate** | Agentic development applied to scientific pursuits |
| **Summative** | The capstone Project — no sit-down exam |

## The two threads

The course is held together by two through-lines that run from week 1 to the capstone:

- **Evaluation becomes fitness** — a progression of 2-week projects from agentic development
  to evolutionary frameworks.
- **Context engineering** — a week-1 concept that deepens into retrieval, memory, and
  domain knowledge.

See the [skill progression](PROGRESSION.md) for the full capability ladder.
